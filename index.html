FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you call.

Is the site visit and quotation free?

Yes. We visit your site, discuss your plans and send a detailed quotation at no charge.

How long does building a house take?

A typical 3-4 bedroom home takes 6 to 9 months depending on design, weather and approvals. We agree a schedule before we start.

Do you help with building permits and drawings?

Yes. We coordinate architects, engineers and approvals so the process is handled for you.

Do you offer a warranty?

All our work carries a defects-liability period and structural warranty, detailed in your contract.

Can I pay in stages?

Yes. Payments are tied to completed milestones, so you only pay for work delivered.
